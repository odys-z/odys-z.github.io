致谢
====

个人文档同步工具 Portfolio 基于以下开源项目和免费软件构建：

- JDK 1.8, 17

- Tomcat & Docker

- `Antlr 4 <https://github.com/antlr/antlr4>`_，BSD-3-Clause 许可证

- Jetty Server 12 & Jetty EE8 12

- Apache Daemon

- Apache Common Libs, Common IO, `Common Validators <https://github.com/apache/commons-validator>`_.

- `Exiftool <https://exiftool.org/index.html#license>`_，作者 `Phil Harvey <https://exiftool.org/index.html#license>`_，采用 `免费许可证 <https://exiftool.org/index.html#license>`_。

- React.js

- Node.js

- `Material-UI (React) 4 <https://v4.mui.com/>`_

- `Mozilla PDF.js <https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js>`_，版权所有 2016 Mozilla Foundation

- `Zxing <https://zxing.org/w/decode.jspx>`_

- `MultiType-FilePicker (Android) <https://github.com/fishwjy/MultiType-FilePicker>`_

- `Image Viewer, PhotoView <https://github.com/bm-x/PhotoView>`_

- `Image Loader, Glide <https://github.com/bumptech/glide>`_

- `Slint <https://github.com/slint-ui/slint>`_ & `SurrealismUI <https://github.com/Surrealism-All/SurrealismUI>`_
